      How it works

      The control unit reads the opcode and turns on one operation line. The ALU uses the matching unit and the answer is shown on the monitor. Registers keep their values; change them in the Registers panel.

      Flags

      ZF zero: result is 0 · CF carry out of bit 7 · OF signed overflow · SF sign: bit 7 is 1 · PF parity: even number of 1 bits
